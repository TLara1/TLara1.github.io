### The Stokes Equations
We begin with the Stokes equations,
\begin{equation}
-\partial_i p +\mu\partial^2 u_i = -f_i,\quad\partial_iu_i=0.
\end{equation}
$$u_i$$ is the velocity field, $$p$$ is the pressure field, and $$f_i$$ is a non-conservative force distribution. $$\mu$$ is the fluid viscosity. The equation can also be written as the divergence of a stress tensor,
\begin{equation}
\partial_j\sigma_{ij} = -f_i,\quad\sigma_{ij}=-p\delta_{ij}+2\mu\e_{ij},
\end{equation}
where $$e_{ij}$$ are the components of the symmetric rate of deformation tensor,
\begin{equation}
\e_{ij}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\partial_i u_j + \partial_j u_i.
\end{equation}
